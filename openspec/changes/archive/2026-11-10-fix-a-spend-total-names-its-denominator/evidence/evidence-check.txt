## What the four were

Located with a pattern search over the change directory (`ctx_grep` pattern `§7`, run before the correction); the
lines as they stood then:

    ./tasks.md:43:## 3. The guideline — `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md` §7b
    ./design.md:39:- `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md` §7b: one sentence.
    ./proposal.md:47:**Owning guideline section:** `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md` §7b "What an item costs, and the bounds on it", the
    ./proposal.md:68:4. **`guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md` §7b gains the rule** the two figures follow, with the measurement.

Each was a **guideline section**, not a backlog `§`-issue: `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md:254` `## §7b What an item
costs, and the bounds on it`. The check's pattern is `§[0-9]+` (`tools/loop/evidence-check.sh:68`), so it read the
`§7` inside `§7b` as a citation of the migration's `§ → issue` numbering, where no row 7 exists — two namespaces the
check does not tell apart. The class is pre-existing at this base and outside this change (the same report is in
`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-06-loop-suite-cost-is-reported/evidence/evidence-check.txt`).

## The correction

The four citations were repointed at the same section by line, dropping the `§` the check misreads:
`guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md:254` (`## §7b What an item costs, and the bounds on it`), verified with
`tools/look.sh '^## ' --path guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md`. No claim, figure or observation changed — only the
form of the four pointers. The gate result this report cites was copied beside the evidence it sits with:
`evidence/gate-run.txt`.
