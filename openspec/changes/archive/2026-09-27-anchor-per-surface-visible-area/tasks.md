## 9. Defect correction (2026-09-16): collide-only resolution (device report — vehicle left of canvas center)

The shipped rescale mapping (task 1.1) moves EVERY preset into the reduced visible rect, shifting the
default center/center preset left (right widget column) and up (bottom card / free-driving pill) on the
phone; on-device portrait navigation shows the vehicle a fraction of the width left of center with default
anchors. Fix: collision-remap semantics (design D1) — a preset resolves to its exact fraction unless its
marker footprint + padding overlaps a measured covered region; only then it moves per axis to the nearest
free position (covered-region edge + the grid's 10% margin), result clamped to `0.1..0.9`. The AA host-pane
path is the same rule specialized to left/right (design D6) and its behavior is unchanged.
