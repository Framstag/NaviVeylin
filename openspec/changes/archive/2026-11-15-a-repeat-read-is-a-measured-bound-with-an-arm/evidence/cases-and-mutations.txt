`repeat#aRepeatReadIsCountedAndTheRefusalArmWithholdsIt` drives one process through `ctx_read`: a path read, the same
path read again, the path appended to (an `EDITED-LINE` marker) and read again under `refuse`, and a pair under a
value the rule does not declare. `adapter#theChildIsStartedWithTheRepeatReadArm` copies
`tools/pi-child-events.sh`, `tools/pi-trim-result.mjs` and `tools/pi-shell-refusal.mjs` into a scratch project, forces
`refuse`, and then records a word the rule does not declare — the child must be handed `measure` for it.
`report#theRuleRecordsTheSecondReadsItCounted` writes three item directories (31, 32, 33) whose records hold
`LOOP-TOOL-RESULT-REPEAT-READ: ctx_read turn=4 arm=measure` and `turn=6 arm=refuse`, the second item's directory
holding no record at all, and reads the report's clause against them.
