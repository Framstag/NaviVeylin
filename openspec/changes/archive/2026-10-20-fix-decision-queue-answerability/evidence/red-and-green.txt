## 4. The assertion that the removal fires only when the label is there

`tools/issues/close.sh` passes `--remove-label agent/needs-decision` only when the item carries it. Verified by
mutation in the session: inserting an unconditional `remove_args=(--remove-label "agent/needs-decision")` after
`remove_args=()` makes `close#writesTheSettledStatus` fail with
`an item without the decision label must not be written as if it carried one`, and the restore is green again.
