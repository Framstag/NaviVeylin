# Correction round 2 — what the review refused, and what was changed

Review round 1 (`evidence/review-round-1-requested.md`) carried one blocking finding and four `UNPROVEN` lines.
Its decision block recommended option (b), and that is what this round applies — no new claim, case or assertion.

## The blocking finding, quoted

> BLOCKING: the scenario is one form of three the requirement forbids. The shipped text
> (`openspec/specs/documentation-ownership/spec.md:698`) reads "it SHALL cite each owner as a path of its own … and
> SHALL NOT cite **a glob, a directory or a directory's complete listing** in their place", but class 4 requires an
> asterisk on the line … `owner: .pi/skills/` — a directory cited as the owners' home, with no glob — is silent,
> exits 0, and no scenario covers it.

Options recorded: (a) merge as it stands — rejected; (b) narrow the SHALL NOT to the glob form, leaving
`design.md`'s "the requirement says no more than that" true; (c) add detection of a bare directory citation and a
case for it; (d) abandon the change. The reviewer recommended (b); this round applies (b).

## The change, per file

- `openspec/specs/documentation-ownership/spec.md:698` and this change's
  `specs/documentation-ownership/spec.md` — the requirement now reads: "it SHALL cite each owner as a path of its
  own, the way the removed block named them, and SHALL NOT cite a glob — the form a directory's complete listing
  takes — in their place". One refused form, the one class 4 can see; the scenario's own text already named the
  glob as that form.
- `design.md`, "The fix" → "The rule" — "never as a glob — the form a directory's complete listing takes".
- `proposal.md`, `### Modified Capabilities` — the same wording, so the proposal's capability line matches the
  requirement it lands.
- `tasks.md` §6 — this round's three tasks, each with its verification.

## The four `UNPROVEN` lines: deleted, not supported

- `proposal.md` — "the OpenSpec CLI validates the change and exits 0" removed from the root-cause sentence.
- `design.md` — the root cause's "no tool reads the record" and the same CLI claim removed; it now reads "The claim
  is uncheckable from the record: its reader is given a set of 35 where the claim is about eight".
- `tools/check-openspec-hygiene.sh`, class 4 header — "The CLI validates the change and exits 0, so" removed; the
  class paragraph keeps its reason and its citation of issue #732.
- `tasks.md` 2.2 — "the case's clean sibling … passes on base" removed; the task now states only what the record
  holds: the case extends case `a`'s clean tree, and that tree passes once the class lands.
- `proposal.md` — "The claim — each skill's job is stated in its own contract — is true" removed; the sentence now
  states only that the record does not show the claim for the owners its block was about.
