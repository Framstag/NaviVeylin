## What the three were

**Two `§7` (C1).** `tasks.md:34` and `design.md:47` pointed at the guideline section as `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md`
§7c. The check reads `§[0-9]\+` (`tools/loop/evidence-check.sh:68`), so it took the `§7` inside `§7c` as a citation of
the migration's `§ → issue` numbering, where no row 7 exists — two namespaces the check does not tell apart. The class
is pre-existing at this base and outside this change; the correction is the one the archive already carries
(`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-06-loop-suite-cost-is-reported/evidence/evidence-check.txt`): repoint the two
citations at the same section **by line**, dropping the `§` the check misreads —
`guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md`:530 "The gate and its suite". No claim, figure or observation changed.

**One quoted tally (C2).** `tasks.md:47` quotes the gate's aggregate line `LOOP-GATE: tests=878 failures=0 errors=0`.
`gate.sh` writes one XML per step under `app/build/test-results/loop-gate` and no aggregate of its own, so no single
XML carried `tests="878"`. Two corrections, both of them the gate's own numbers and neither of them a re-run:

- `evidence/gate-tallies/` — the 26 per-step XMLs the run wrote, copied out of `app/build/test-results/loop-gate`
  before a later run can overwrite them, and checkable: the seven steps that carry cases sum to the aggregate
  `348 + 266 + 160 + 34 + 34 + 29 + 7 = 878`, `failures="0"`, `errors="0"`.
- `evidence/gate-summary.xml` — the transcription of the aggregate line, the form the archive already keeps for a
  tally `gate.sh` does not write itself
  (`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-07-fix-report-splits-the-spend-by-phase/evidence/gate-summary.xml`).

C2's one pair is that aggregate line, and it is the only quoted `tests=`/`failures=` pair the change's markdown
carries: the red case's own numbers reach the artifacts through `evidence/red-run.log` and the suite's own summary
(`loop-selftest: 1 of 1 cases failed`), not through a JUnit tally, because this shell suite writes no XML.
