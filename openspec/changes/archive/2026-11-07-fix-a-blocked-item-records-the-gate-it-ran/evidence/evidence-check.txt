## What the six were

Three C1s: `§7d` in `proposal.md:65` and `design.md:80`, and `§7e` in `design.md:85`. Each is a **guideline section**,
not a backlog `§`-issue: `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md`:710 "Refusals, records and the landing" and `:892`. The
check's pattern is `§[0-9]\+` (`tools/loop/evidence-check.sh:68`), so it read the `§7` inside `§7d`/`§7e` as a
citation of the migration's `§ → issue` numbering, where no row 7 exists — two namespaces the check does not tell
apart. Repointed at the same sections by line, dropping the `§` the check misreads; no claim changed.

Three C2s, on two shapes:

- `LOOP-GATE: tests=880 failures=2 errors=0` — issue #798's own gate line, quoted from another item's tree before this
  work. No XML of that run is in this change's evidence, and it is not a tally this change took: the line now carries
  "not retained as a tally here" beside the numbers.
- `tests=1 failures=2` — twice, in the *expected string* inside `run#aRedGateIsBlockedByTheTail`'s failure message
  (`proposal.md`, `evidence/evidence.md`). That is neither a tally nor a run: the case's own `tests=1 failures=1` is the
  red tally, and `evidence/red-on-HEAD.xml` carries it. The quote now carries "not retained as one" beside it.
