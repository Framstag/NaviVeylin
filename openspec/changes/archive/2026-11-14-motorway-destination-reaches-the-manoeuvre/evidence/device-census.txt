one `dest_empty` (route B, step 10) is an A 2 -> A 3 change whose way carries no destination tag in
this extract: the wording has no destination clause on the **patched** build either, so the count is
a legitimate absence, not the loss the fix repairs — `dest_empty` cannot tell the two apart, because
both are "no record on the step".

therefore not reproduced by real data on either route, on either build. What stands behind the
change is the case in `RouteStepModelTest`, which is red on the pin and green on the commit.
