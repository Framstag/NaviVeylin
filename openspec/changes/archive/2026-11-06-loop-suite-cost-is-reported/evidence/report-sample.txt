# The report, as the change's driver prints it — base 0fb7a80, change root, 2026-11-06

Every line below is a command's own output; the command is above it.

## A suite, one group at --jobs 4

    $ bash tools/loop-selftest.sh --group driver --jobs 4
    …
    loop-selftest: driver 22 cases 98.87 s
    loop-selftest: all 22 cases passed (JDK 27, offline, no token, no sandbox) — 32.28 s, selection: --group driver

A group's figure is the sum of its cases' own elapsed times; the selection's is the run's wall clock. At `--jobs 4`
the 22 cases paid 98.87 s of case time inside 32.28 s of wall clock.

## A passing case's line, without and with --times

    $ bash tools/loop-selftest.sh --case 'driver#theListIsFilteredTheSameWayASuiteRunIs'
    PASS driver#theListIsFilteredTheSameWayASuiteRunIs

…and nothing else: one named `--case` with no pattern keeps the shape the mutation harness and the leftovers cases
parse — the `PASS` line and the exit status, no summary.

    $ bash tools/loop-selftest.sh --group driver --case 'driver#theList*' --times
    PASS driver#theListIsFilteredTheSameWayASuiteRunIs (1.22 s)
    loop-selftest: driver 1 cases 1.22 s
    loop-selftest: all 1 cases passed (JDK 27, offline, no token, no sandbox) — 1.85 s, selection: --group driver --case driver#theList*

With `--times` the figure is added to the case's own line and the name the line is read for is unchanged.
