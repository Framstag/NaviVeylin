## What the five were

    $ grep -rn '§7' openspec/changes/fix-gate-independent-steps-run-together
    ./proposal.md:29:**Owning guideline.** `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md` §7c "The gate and its suite" — the section that states the
    ./proposal.md:58:4. **`guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md` §7c — the rule beside its measurement.**

Three guideline citations, not backlog `§`-issues: the check's pattern is `§[0-9]\+`
(`tools/loop/evidence-check.sh:68`), so it read the `§7` inside `§7c` as a citation of the migration's
`§ → issue` numbering, where no row 7 exists — the same two namespaces the archive's
`2026-11-06-loop-suite-cost-is-reported/evidence/evidence-check.txt` names, and the same correction: the three
pointers now name the section by file and line, `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md`:501 "The gate and its suite".
No claim, figure or observation changed — only the form of the three pointers.

The two C2 failures were the probe's `verdict=` lines, quoted inside a fenced block in `evidence.md`:
`PROBE pool=<n> … verdict=LOOP-GATE: tests=3 failures=0 …`. They are a throwaway repository's summed verdict, and
its tally directory is deleted with that repository, so no XML on disk carries that summed pair. The block now
quotes the lines whose facts are retained (the two wall clocks and each probe's `tallies=` line) and states in
prose that both verdicts carried the three suites with no failure; the raw verdict lines stay in
`evidence/pool-probe.log`, which is not scanned for tallies.
