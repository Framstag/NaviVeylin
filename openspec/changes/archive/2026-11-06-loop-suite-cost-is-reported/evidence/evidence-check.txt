## What the six were

    $ grep -rn '§7' openspec/changes/loop-suite-cost-is-reported
    ./proposal.md:20:... 2026-10-25, `guidelines/Backlog.md` §7c), against 312 cases paying **17 min 15 s** on the
    ./proposal.md:49:- **`guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md`** §7c — the paragraph that states the suite's cost gains the report
    ./proposal.md:55:  on the same host (`guidelines/Backlog.md` §7c: 8m25s, 9m46s, 5m45s for the same gate), so the report
    ./design.md:42:  spent 30 minutes at 194 MB free RAM (`guidelines/Backlog.md` §7c, the measurement behind `LOOP_MIN_FREE_MIB`).
    ./design.md:48:  is what a person and the gate read. `guidelines/Backlog.md` §7c already states that a suite's numbers
    ./tasks.md:58:      `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md` §7c states the rule with these numbers.

Each was a **guideline section**, not a backlog `§`-issue: `guidelines/Backlog.md` §7c "The gate and its suite"
(`:276`, the routing stub) and `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md` §7c "The gate and its suite" (`:458`, the body).
The check's pattern is `§[0-9]\+` (`tools/loop/evidence-check.sh:68`), so it read the `§7` inside `§7c` as a
citation of the migration's `§ → issue` numbering, where no row 7 exists — two namespaces the check does not
tell apart. The class is pre-existing at this base and outside this change: the same run over two open sibling
changes reports it too —

    $ bash tools/loop/evidence-check.sh openspec/changes/loop-suite-legs-across-runners --root .
    4    # lines: FAIL C1 dangling §7
    $ bash tools/loop/evidence-check.sh openspec/changes/fix-remote-heads-are-swept --root .
    5    # lines: FAIL C1 dangling §7

## The correction

The six citations were repointed at the same sections by line, keeping the files the review of this item asked
for (`Backlog.recipes.md` named as the file the paragraph is in) and dropping the `§` that the check misreads:
`guidelines/Backlog.md`:276 "The gate and its suite" and `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md`:458 "The gate and its
suite". No claim, figure or observation changed — only the form of the six pointers.
