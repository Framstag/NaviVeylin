No log line names the incoming action and no test covers `onStartCommand` or the provider's callback routing. Verify: tapping `Stop` on the phone shade ends navigation, withdraws the notification and leaves the route panel non-navigating, with a car session live in the same process; add the provider-routing + service-action regression tests and log the action in `onStartCommand`.
