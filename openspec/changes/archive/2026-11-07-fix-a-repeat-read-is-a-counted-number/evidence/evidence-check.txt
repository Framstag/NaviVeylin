## What the five were

The five citations (`proposal.md` two, `design.md` one, `tasks.md` two) were **guideline sections**,
`guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md` §7b "What an item costs, and the bounds on it" (`:254`) and §7e "Leftovers, the
report and the queue" (`:892`), not backlog `§`-issue citations. The check's pattern is `§[0-9]\+`
(`tools/loop/evidence-check.sh:68`), so it read the `§7` inside `§7b`/`§7e` as the migration's `§ → issue` numbering,
where no row 7 exists — two namespaces the check does not tell apart (the same class the tail of
`2026-11-06-fix-an-adoption-the-tree-refuses-is-recorded` corrected, `evidence/evidence-check.txt` there).

## The correction

The five citations were repointed at the same sections by line, dropping the `§` the check misreads:
`guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md`:254 and `guidelines/Backlog.recipes.md`:892. No claim, figure or observation changed
— only the form of the five pointers.
