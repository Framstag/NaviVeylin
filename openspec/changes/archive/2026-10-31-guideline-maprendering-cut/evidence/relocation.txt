# Relocation — the cut is a move, not a rewrite

The text was moved by line slice (`sed -n`), never retyped, so every moved byte is identical. The proof
is the per-section cost: for §12, §13, §14 and §19 the post-cut `--sizes` byte count equals the pre-cut
one to the byte.

A section's cost runs from its `## ` heading to the line before the next one, so the slice carries the
separator line that closes it (`---`); that is why §14 and §19 end their new document with one.

`§20` is 3 036 B in its new home where the source region measured 3 044: **−2 B** are the two trailing
blank lines the source region counted and a file end does not need, and **−6 B** are the one name-only
citation retarget inside the section (`MapRendering.recipes.md` §20 → `MapStyling.why.md` §20, since the
anchor it names moved with it). No statement, sentence or example changed.

## The arithmetic closes

unnumbered tail 519.

Post-cut `MapRendering.md` = 30 344 = preamble 657 + §1…§5 29 045 + the unnumbered tail 642.

46 751 − 16 604 (moved out) = 30 147; + 53 (preamble rewritten for the new homes) + 21 (§1b and §5's
separators) + 123 (the "Sections that moved out of this document" block grows by two entries) = **30 344**.

Carried: 16 604 out, 16 596 landed (10 505 + 3 055 + 3 036) — the 8 B difference is the 2 trailing blank
lines and the 6 B name-only citation retarget above.
